At a glance

Magic Tree keeps your tree compositions and selected photos on your device. The app does not upload them to a developer-operated server. Premium payments are processed by Apple. There is no developer-operated account or cloud-sync service in the app.

About this policy

Magic Tree is an iOS app for decorating Christmas trees with ornaments, personal photos, memories, milestones, stickers and text. This policy covers the app and these Privacy and Support pages. The developer/publisher responsible for Magic Tree is Kateryna Honcharova.

Photos and tree creations

You choose which images to add through the iOS image chooser. The app reads the selected image on your device and keeps a local copy with your composition. Photo memories may be resized for editing; custom image decorations may retain their original image data and metadata. Names, dates, messages, decoration positions, widget framing settings and other text you add are stored as part of the composition.

The app does not automatically browse or upload your entire photo library. If the iOS chooser offers a camera option and you choose it, camera access is handled by iOS. Saving a wallpaper or widget preview asks for permission to add the resulting image to Photos. Original images in your library are not deleted by editing a tree.

App preferences and compositions are saved in local web storage within the app. Selected images can be embedded in those saved records. App artwork and fonts are bundled with the app; editing does not require a remote font or image service.

Premium purchases through Apple

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. Apple StoreKit supplies product information and the localized price, handles payment and purchase restoration, and provides verified transaction information to the app. The developer does not receive your payment-card details from the app and does not operate a payment server.

The app uses transaction information on your device to determine Premium access. A shared Keychain record for widgets includes the product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase environment, access status and last verification time. This record is not sent to a developer-operated server. StoreKit communications with Apple and Apple's purchase records are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

To restore a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Home Screen widgets

When you save a tree or choose Update Home Screen Widget in Widget Preview, the app renders Small, Medium and Large widget images and stores them, together with basic composition metadata, in an on-device App Group container shared with the Magic Tree widget extension. These images can include any photos, names or messages you placed on your tree.

The widget reads these local images and the shared Premium access record without a developer-operated server. People who can see your Home Screen may see the content of a widget. iOS controls widget refresh timing; removing content from the editor does not necessarily update an already displayed widget immediately.

Sharing, diagnostics and other services

The app has no integrated analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, remote crash-reporting SDK or developer-operated remote storage. It does not ask for location, contacts or microphone access. The developer does not receive your compositions, selected photos, usage events or local Premium record through an app-operated collection service.

Native widget loading can write revision identifiers and error messages to Apple's local system logs. iOS, the App Store and Apple's optional diagnostics or device-backup services have their own settings and practices. Apple may make app distribution, sales or diagnostic reports available through its developer services. Developer use of these reports: The developer may use Apple-provided aggregated sales, purchase and diagnostic reports available through App Store Connect to maintain and improve Magic Tree. These are Apple-provided reports, not a separate device-analytics or tracking service integrated by the developer..

If you save an image to Photos, your Photos or iCloud Photos settings may sync it through Apple's services. Device backups, including iCloud Backup where enabled and applicable, may also contain copies of local app data. These are system-managed behaviors, not a Magic Tree cloud-backup service. If you choose to share it with another person or service, their handling is outside this app. Magic Tree itself does not implement cloud backup or cross-device composition sync.

Retention and your controls

  • Saved compositions stay on the device until you edit or replace them, use Start Over for that tree, or the app's local data is removed. Other tree designs are separate.
  • Removing a photo or text from a composition does not delete an already exported PNG or an older widget image. Undo history may temporarily keep earlier versions during editing. Older legacy composition records can also remain in local storage; there is no complete legacy-data purge control.
  • Start Over does not automatically erase an already published widget image. Save the updated tree or use Update Home Screen Widget to replace the active widget composition; iOS may continue showing a cached view until it refreshes. Older image generations may remain temporarily in shared storage; eligible older files are cleaned up on a later save, not on a guaranteed deletion schedule.
  • Delete exported images separately in Photos, including Recently Deleted where applicable. Remove a Home Screen widget if you do not want it displayed.
  • You can review or change Photos and camera access in iOS Settings. The exact permission options depend on your iOS version.

Deleting the app normally removes its app-container data, but offloading the app, device backups, shared storage and Keychain records have different system-managed lifetimes. The app has no all-data-erasure button or remote deletion service. Premium Keychain information may remain after app deletion; Apple keeps its own purchase history. There is no daily expiry for a verified lifetime Premium widget record.

The developer cannot access or remotely recover your local tree designs. Device loss, storage limits or deleting app data can cause loss of compositions. A saved PNG is an image, not a restorable editable tree backup.

This website and support messages

These pages do not contain analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, contact forms or social embeds. The site code does not set cookies or use browser storage. This does not mean visiting a hosted page involves no data processing.

This site is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting request processing is separate from the app's on-device photo storage; this site does not receive your tree compositions.

If you choose to email magictree.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your sender address, message and any attachments you send. Email also involves your own email provider and Google/Gmail, which hosts this support address. See Google's Privacy Policy. Send only what is needed for your question, and avoid private photos, passwords or payment details.

Support email retention: Support emails are handled through Gmail and retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide support, and meet applicable legal obligations. No fixed retention period has been established. You may email magictree.support@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence held by the developer; this does not erase local app data or records held separately by Apple or email providers.

Questions and privacy requests

Contact Kateryna Honcharova at magictree.support@gmail.com for support or a privacy question. Where applicable, you may ask about or request deletion of support correspondence held by the developer. Local app content must be managed on your device because the developer does not hold a copy.

Changes to the app or these practices will be reflected in this policy and its Last Updated date. For help using the app, visit Magic Tree Support.
