Frequently asked questions

How does Premium work?

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple. It unlocks Premium trees, ornaments and toppers; it is not a subscription. Check the localized price shown in the app before confirming. Free features remain available without Premium.

How do I restore Premium?

Open a Premium tree or item to reach the Premium panel, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection and Apple's sign-in or verification may be needed. Restoring a purchase does not restore your local tree compositions.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles purchase refunds and eligibility. Request help at reportaproblem.apple.com. Magic Tree support cannot issue an Apple refund directly.

How do I save a wallpaper?

In the editor, tap Save, then Save Image. Allow Magic Tree to add images to Photos when iOS asks. The app saves a static 2048 × 3657 PNG. You can then select that image as a wallpaper in iOS. If saving fails, check Photos access and available device storage, then try again.

How do Home Screen widgets work?

Save your tree in the editor, or use Update Home Screen Widget in Widget Preview, to update its widget images. Drag and zoom in Widget Preview to adjust the crop separately for each widget size. On the Home Screen, use iOS's Add Widget controls, search for Magic Tree and choose Small, Medium or Large. Native Magic Tree widgets require iOS 18 or later. iOS controls when changes appear; updates may not be immediate. The in-app Widget Preview is a separate preview and image-saving feature.

Verified lifetime Premium widgets do not require daily app launches. If access needs verification, open Magic Tree and use Restore Purchases if necessary. Apple purchase-status changes are applied when the app learns of them.

Where are my photos and designs stored?

Compositions and selected image copies are stored locally on your device. Widget images are shared locally with the widget extension. Magic Tree does not provide an account-based cloud backup or cross-device sync. Exported PNGs stay in Photos under your own Photos settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Can support recover a lost tree or erase all local data?

Support does not have a server copy of your creations and cannot recover or remotely erase them. You can remove decorations or use Start Over to clear the current tree; other tree designs remain saved. Start Over does not clear published widget images, all legacy records or Keychain data. There is no combined all-data-erasure control. Delete exported images separately in Photos and save an updated tree to update its widgets. Deleting the app normally removes app-container data, while device backups, shared storage and Keychain records can have different lifetimes. Offloading is not the same as deleting.

What should I include in a support request?

Describe what happened and what you expected. Your iOS version and Magic Tree version, if known, can help. A screenshot is optional; remove personal photos, names and other private information first. Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials or full payment details. There is no need to send your complete tree composition.
